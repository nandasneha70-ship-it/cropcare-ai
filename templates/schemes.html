🌾 Government Agriculture Schemes

Explore welfare programs, crop insurances, and chemical-free cultivation subsidies sponsored by the Government to assist farmers.

Organic Farming Support

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)

Promotes organic farming through a cluster approach and Participatory Guarantee System (PGS) certification. Farmers receive financial support of Rs. 50,000 per hectare over 3 years, with 62% of the amount allocated for organic inputs like bio-fertilizers and botanical pest controls.

Visit Official Site →
Direct Income Support

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

An initiative by the Government of India that guarantees direct income support of Rs. 6,000 per year in three equal installments to all landholding farmer families across the country to cover agricultural inputs and domestic needs.

Visit Official Site →
Crop Insurance

PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

An act-of-nature crop insurance program covering yield losses due to non-preventable risks such as droughts, floods, pest attacks, and diseases. Farmers pay a minimal premium of just 2% for Kharif crops and 1.5% for Rabi crops.

Visit Official Site →
Soil Management

Soil Health Card Scheme

Assists state governments in issuing soil health cards to all farmers. The card provides details on the nutrient status of the soil, enabling farmers to optimize fertilizer application, improve soil health, and choose the most suitable crops.
