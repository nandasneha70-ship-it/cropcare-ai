📖 Comprehensive Crop Guide

Explore optimal growing conditions, common diseases, and preventive advice for essential crops.

🍅 Vegetable

Tomato

Warm-season vegetable rich in Vitamin C and Lycopene, highly popular in local and global culinary markets.

Suitable Growing Conditions: Sandy loam soil, pH 6.0-7.0, 21°C - 24°C, moderate sun
Water Requirements: 1 - 1.5 inches per week. Irrigate at base.
Common Diseases: Early Blight, Late Blight, Septoria Leaf Spot, Spider Mites
Basic Prevention Tips: Use organic mulch to prevent soil spores splashing. Prune lower foliage.
🥔 Vegetable

Potato

Underground starch-rich tuber crop, serving as a primary staple source of dietary energy globally.

Suitable Growing Conditions: Loose well-drained acidic soil, pH 5.2-6.5, Cool 15°C - 20°C
Water Requirements: Consistent moisture. 1 - 2 inches/week. Reduce before harvest.
Common Diseases: Late Blight, Early Blight, Common Scab, Black Scurf
Basic Prevention Tips: Plant certified disease-free seed tubers. Rotate fields every 3 years.
🌾 Grain

Rice (Paddy)

Major cereal grass grain crop, widely cultivated in waterlogged fields across tropical regions.

Suitable Growing Conditions: Clayey alluvial soils, pH 5.5-6.5, Warm humid 22°C - 32°C
Water Requirements: High. Flooded conditions (5 - 10 cm depth) during growth.
Common Diseases: Rice Blast, Bacterial Leaf Blight, Sheath Blight
Basic Prevention Tips: Avoid excessive Nitrogen fertilizer. Plow or burn crop residues post-harvest.
🌾 Grain

Wheat

Major winter cereal food grain crop, critical for bread-making and primary nutrition in cool climates.

Suitable Growing Conditions: Well-drained fertile clay loams, pH 6.0-7.5, Cool 10°C - 22°C
Water Requirements: Moderate. Requires 4 - 6 timely irrigations at critical growth stages.
Common Diseases: Leaf Rust, Yellow Rust, Black Stem Rust, Loose Smut
Basic Prevention Tips: Sow rust-resistant varieties. Treat seeds with biological protectants.
🌽 Grain

Maize (Corn)

Coarse grain feed and cereal crop, offering high adaptability across varying agro-climatic zones.

Suitable Growing Conditions: Rich loamy soil, pH 5.8-7.5, Warm sunny 21°C - 27°C
Water Requirements: Moderate (500-800 mm). High sensitivity to waterlogging.
Common Diseases: Common Rust, Northern Leaf Blight, Turcicum Leaf Blight
Basic Prevention Tips: Adopt deep summer plowing. Maintain clean fields with regular weeding.
☁️ Cash Crop

Cotton

Major commercial fiber cash crop, grown extensively in heavy black soils (regur soil) of central regions.

Suitable Growing Conditions: Deep black clay soil, pH 6.0-8.0, Hot dry 21°C - 30°C
Water Requirements: Moderate. Irrigation required primarily during flowering and boll opening.
Common Diseases: Boll Rot, Fusarium Wilt, Bacterial Leaf Blight
Basic Prevention Tips: Prune dense lower branches. Utilize crop rotation and pest-resistant Bt seeds.
🍎 Fruit

Apple

Premium temperate orchard fruit crop, requiring winter chilling hours to induce quality fruit set.

Suitable Growing Conditions: Well-drained loamy hillsides, pH 6.0-6.8, Cold winters
Water Requirements: Consistent moderate soil moisture. Regular watering during fruit growth.
Common Diseases: Apple Scab, Black Rot, Cedar Apple Rust, Fire Blight
Basic Prevention Tips: Prune branches yearly for airflow. Clear fallen orchard foliage.
🍇 Fruit

Grape

Vignicultural fruit crop cultivated on trellises, used for table consumption, raisins, and wine production.

Suitable Growing Conditions: Gravelly sandy loam, pH 6.5-7.5, Warm dry climate
Water Requirements: Low-Moderate. Drip irrigation is highly recommended to avoid leaf mold.
Common Diseases: Downy Mildew, Powdery Mildew, Black Rot, Esca
Basic Prevention Tips: Train vines for structural spacing. Spray organic copper solutions preventatively.
