🌿 About the Project

This application is a complete, working implementation of the system outlined in the research paper: "Development of a Plant Disease Detection System Using Deep Learning and Organic Solutions for Sustainable Agriculture" (ICCSAI 2025).

🔬 Deep Learning Framework

Our neural network architecture leverages Transfer Learning on top of the MobileNetV2 backbone, pre-trained on ImageNet.

  • Optimized Input size: Leaf photos are scaled to 224x224 pixels.
  • Fully Connected Dense Layers: Includes dropout layers (0.3 and 0.2) to prevent overfitting on smaller dataset subsets.
  • Optimization: Uses the Adam optimizer and Categorical Cross-Entropy loss.
  • Two-Pass Training: In phase 1, we freeze the backbone and train the top classification head. In phase 2, we unfreeze top backbone layers for fine-tuning at a lower learning rate.

🌱 Why Organic Solutions?

Traditional farming relies heavily on synthetic chemical pesticides, leading to soil degradation, water toxicity, and pesticide resistance. Our database maps classifications to organic treatments:

Neem Oil & Leaves

Naturally disrupts the life cycle of insect pests and fungi.

Cow Urine Solution

Rich in nitrogen and natural antimicrobial proteins that boost immunity.

Turmeric & Garlic

Contains curcumin and allicin which act as potent natural fungicides.
